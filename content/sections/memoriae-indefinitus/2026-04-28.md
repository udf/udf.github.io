I got to the train station early this morning and the sun had just risen, I could see a spiral galaxy next to the sun somehow. I went to take a picture of it, but I noticed that I had brought my old phone instead (not sure how because I uninstalled Uber so I would not make this mistake). I took a picture anyways and then I booked an Uber back home to get my new phone (why would this be important enough to go all the way back home?). When I got home and grabbed the correct phone, then I heard a loud sound coming from outside - my dad broke the gate down but luckily I escaped without him seeing me. My Uber back to the train station was a canoe with wheels (I had accidentally selected the "Oceanic" option. It was fast enough to leave before my dad noticed, however it was terrifying being so low to the ground while weaving between traffic.
