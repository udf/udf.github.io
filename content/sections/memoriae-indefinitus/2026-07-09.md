I was awoken by my dad helping my granddad with his car outside at like 4 AM, I think my granddad was leaving to go back home (a long drive, so he'd leave early to make it home on time). It took a while and my dad had left the garage door unlocked. Once my granddad had left, I helped my dad lock the gates, but then we heard rustling coming from the garage. we ran inside the house after spotting a human shaped figure coming towards us but my dad was unable to close the door in time and a couple of guys spotted us. The leader saw me and said "Sammy, my girl, we've found you and it's all over now". He then pulled out a syringe and injected it into my arm. It burned a lot and then I died I think, but I reincarnated as someone else, probably in a different timeline because it seemed like a desert village. a similar thing happened with some guys finding me, mentioning that I'm Sammy and then injecting me with something which kills me.

After that I was stitch from _Lilo and Stitch_ and someone was chasing after me after they spotted me at a supermarket. I managed to evade them by running into one of the backroom areas. It looked like a warehouse with lots of shelves filled with various goods on palettes. There were these small electric forklift type vehicles which were carrying leaves in a basket on their rear. I killed one of the drivers and hopped into one. Luckily it was full so I had the excuse to drive to the exit. It had a very small battery capacity so I had to tap the breaks to use the regen breaking to get some battery back. Somehow a brief break tap was enough to charge the whole battery, I think it was a perpetual motion machine because the warehouse floor was level so there's no way that could've worked. I managed to get to exit which was a large rolling door which was open. I heard an announcement about a stolen woodwork vehicle so I ditched the car and ran outside, climbing over some shelves. There was a steep grassy hill between me and my supposed freedom so I ran up it on all fours to prevent slipping. I then climbed into a dump truck and presumably managed to get away.
